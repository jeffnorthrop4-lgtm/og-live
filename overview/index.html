OG Overview

More resilient lines, delivered as seed.

Oyster Genomics is the seed company for ocean protein. Disease turns a season of investment into an uncertain harvest. OG is working to improve that foundation: more resilient lines, designed for the realities of commercial aquaculture. Better genetics can reach farms through the seed they already buy.

Switchpoint, our platform, finds candidate control points in innate immunity, tests them, and translates what holds up into improved lines.

The product is a more resilient oyster line, delivered through triploid seed. The first species is the Eastern oyster. Growers get improved biology through hatchery partners. The business is proprietary broodstock and recurring seed production, with no direct farm operation.

Oysters are the start. Another species needs its own discovery, validation, and commercial path.
